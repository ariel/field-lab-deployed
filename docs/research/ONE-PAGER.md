# Shoot. Copy. Check. Keep.

Two card pockets. Drive 1 is the working copy; Drive 2 follows it. No field forms.

## Prepare at home

Bring enough cards for the day, two independent drives, a reader and power. Label cards A01, A02, B01, S01. Cleared cards go in READY; recorded or uncertain cards go in USED.

Rehearse the saved setup: OffShoot copies whole cards to Drive 1 with Source & Destination verification; CCC's “Update backup drive” task copies Drive 1 to Drive 2 with read-back verification and recovery snapshots. Keep internal card files unchanged. Use a unique folder such as `Film/20261028/A01/`. Each card is used once that day.

## Shoot

Concentrate on picture and sound. Use your tested sync method. Removed cards go in USED, even partly full. Never format in the field. If READY runs out, stop recording.

An optional voice note is enough: “Pump repair. Keep Maya's pause. B's gauge shot was later. Need clean pump sound.”

## Copy and check

At a safe, stationary break or evening base, check the card label and destination. OffShoot copies the whole card to its own folder on Drive 1. Wait for successful verification; keep its automatic reports.

Then run CCC's “Update backup drive.” Wait for its copy and verification to succeed. Until then, Drive 2 does not protect the new footage. Play important copied picture and sound; brief playback cannot check every frame.

If conditions are unsuitable, secure the card and defer copying. Any interruption, warning or uncertainty: keep the card in USED, preserve successful copies and resolve the problem safely. Do not erase anything to tidy up.

## Organize only when useful

On downtime days, organize folders in Finder on Drive 1 only, keeping complete card packages intact. Finish the batch, preview CCC's changes, then run “Update backup drive” and check success. Do not organize while either app is copying. Organizing before Premiere import avoids later relinking.

## Keep

Eject safely. Cards stay in USED even after successful copies. Before leaving, account for every camera/sound card and both drives; check essential coverage. Carry Drive 2 separately where practical.

## Back at home

Finish missing copies and checks. Before formatting cards, have three verified storage copies that remain afterward: both drives plus a third independent archive, with one kept separately. CCC snapshots provide recovery history, not another independent copy. Then format in the camera/recorder and return cards to READY.

Premiere comes later: review voice notes, sync, add useful markers and make proxies only if needed. Back up the project.
