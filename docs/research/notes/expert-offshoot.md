## Hedge: OffShoot - Getting Started

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UCHBx3iE4ls), published 2025-02-28, 3:06.

Hedge's own tutorial documents the interface. Its claims of superior speed and reliability were not independently tested.

- 0:21-1:28: sets destinations, selects a source and starts a transfer. Covers folders, labels, multiple destinations and adding cards while jobs run.
- 1:38-2:02: recommends at least Source verification and multiple backups for camera-card offloads because the default Transfer method provides less checking.
- 2:07-2:47: demonstrates presets for filtering, renaming and organizing media.
- 2:49-end: points to documentation and support, then closes.

Use this as a short setup lesson. The guide recommends Source & Destination verification and preserving complete camera-card contents. Configure organization around outer roll folders; leave source filenames and required files intact. Card-release approval still needs a separate procedure.

## Documentation check

The [Hedge verification reference](https://docs.hedge.video/offshoot/features/verification) distinguishes Transfer, Source and Source & Destination. It warns against erasing sources after Transfer mode. Source & Destination performs read-back comparisons, although jobs can complete with warnings that require inspection.

The documentation also describes idle-time destination MHL checks in lighter modes. Their completion must be confirmed before relying on them for card release. Selecting Source & Destination explicitly avoids that timing dependency. Existing manifests can supply reference hashes. No installed-app failure test or transfer benchmark was performed.
