# Solo workflow simplification review

Reviewed October 28, 2026 by three subagents taking operator, media-safety and Premiere-editor perspectives. Each reviewed existing material, proposed changes and then read the rewritten field/desk pages. All three accepted the simplified structure, with final wording corrections applied.

The current operating source is ../ONE-PAGER.md; index.html and ONE-PAGER.pdf are generated from it. There is one field routine: Shoot, Copy, Check, Keep. The kit is READY/USED card pockets, sufficient card capacity, two independent drives, reader and power. Cards are used once per shoot date and retained until home. Automatic transfer reports replace manual verification logs. Story notes are optional voice memos.

Removed from field requirements: all CSVs, roll counters, release approval states, metadata IDs/joins, settings snapshots, look registers, proxy/transcript work and Premiere configuration. Prior detailed guides, forms and examples are retained under reference/previous-system with superseded-status notices. A short optional desk page provides preparation, a narrative worked example and postproduction guidance.

The second audit checked interrupted copies, failed/missing drives, repeated filenames across cameras, exhausted card capacity, separate sound, uncertain and partially full cards. Corrections clarify that removing a partly full card still places it in USED, that card shortage requires more capacity or reduced shooting rather than improvised reuse, and that three verified independent storage copies must remain after formatting at home. No application or production-media operations were performed.
